Our Product Selection

Our products are baked in small batches with carefully selected ingredients. Availability may change with the season.

Breads

Our breads are baked in small batches with carefully selected ingredients. Choices may include country loaves, whole-grain breads, and seasonal specialties.

Typical price range: $5-$10 per loaf.

Pastries

Start the morning with handmade pastries, including buttery breakfast pastries, fruit-filled favorites, and rotating seasonal selections.

Our pastry selection is designed for commuters, families, and neighborhood customers looking for a fresh treat.

Typical price range: $3-$7 each.

Cakes

Our celebration cakes are available for birthdays, family gatherings, office events, and other special occasions.

Advance requests are encouraged so our bakery team can discuss availability, size, and other important details.

Typical price range: $30-$75, depending on size and details.
