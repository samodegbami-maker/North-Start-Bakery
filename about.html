About North Star Bakery

Our Beginning

Our Story

North Star Bakery was created to be a warm neighborhood gathering place where customers can enjoy dependable, handmade baked goods and welcoming service.

Our goal is to create a bakery experience that feels friendly and familiar, whether a customer visits every week or is stopping by for the first time.

Thoughtful Ingredients

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We focus on quality ingredients and thoughtful preparation. Whenever practical, we choose ingredients from trusted suppliers and plan seasonal offerings around freshness and availability.

Small-batch preparation helps our team focus on quality, flavor, and presentation.

The People Behind the Bakery

Meet the Team

Our Bakers

Our baking team prepares breads, pastries, and cakes in small batches, with attention to consistency, flavor, and presentation.

The team works throughout the day to provide customers with fresh bakery selections.

Our Front Counter Team

Our customer service team helps guests choose products, answer ingredient questions, and coordinate pre-order requests.

Friendly and helpful service is an important part of the North Star Bakery experience.
